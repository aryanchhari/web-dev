    THERE IS NO SINGLE WINNER

    Every panel has a personality.

    IPS

    The everyday all-rounder.

    A useful place to start for work and mixed use, especially when viewing angles matter.

    VA

    The contrast-minded LCD.

    Often stronger native contrast than IPS. Check dark-transition performance on the actual model.

    TN

    The focused specialist.

    Worth considering for dedicated esports needs. Expect viewing-angle compromises.

    OLED

    The self-lit showstopper.

    Deep blacks and fast pixel transitions. Long-term static use needs extra care.
