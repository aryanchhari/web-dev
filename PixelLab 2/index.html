LIGHT AGAINST DARK

Same white. Different blacks.

Contrast is the difference between the brightest white and the darkest black a screen can produce. Deeper blacks make bright objects stand out and give dark scenes more depth.

IPS LCD

Wide viewing angles. Light from the backlight can make dark areas look grey.

VA LCD

Usually stronger native contrast than conventional IPS or TN, giving dark scenes deeper blacks.

TN LCD

Uses a backlight. Contrast and colours shift more when viewed off-centre.

OLED

Each pixel emits its own light. Black pixels can switch off without a separate backlight.
